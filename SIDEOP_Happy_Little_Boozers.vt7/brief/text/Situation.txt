<font color='#FFA500' size='18'>Ground and Prelims:</font>
<br/>
<br/>Area of operation is a captured Finnish brewery, surrounded by woodlands and connected to the local MSR.
<br/>
<br/>Weather: Dawn
<br/>
<br/><font color='#FFA500' size='18'>Situation – En Forces:</font>
<br/>
<br/>Enemy forces appear to be Russian motorised infantry, likely in platoon strength. The enemy has put in place hasty fortifications, notably on the ASR from the south and the MSR in both directions. 
<br/>Enemy forces are actively patrolling the perimeter of the brewery.
<br/>
<br/><font color='#FFA500' size='18'>Situation – FF</font>
<br/>
<br/>Elements of 3rd Company, 2rd Bn, 3rd Res Bde, consisting of 2 platoons worth of hastily gathered volunteers. No support is available.
<br/>
<br/><font color='#FFA500' size='18'>Mission</font>
<br/>
<br/>Evict Russian forces occupying the Finnish brewery.
<br/>Hold off any possible Russian counter offensive long enough for either official reinforcements to arrive or
<br/>Secure the booze!
