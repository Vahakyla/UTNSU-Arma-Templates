<br/>
<br/><font color='#99cc00'>Commander's Intent</font>
<br/>
<br/>Commander’s intent is to flank the southern checkpoint and then move north in platoon bounding formation, defeating the enemy forces in detail as we encounter them and sending squads to flank any entrenched positions encountered.
<br/>
<br/><font color='#99cc00'>Scheme of Maneuvers</font>
<br/>
<br/>Squads shall use the locally sourced vehicles to transport from our gathering location SE of the brewery to a safe dismount location, outside of the area Russian forces are likely patrolling. 
<br/>
<br/>Units will then form a platoon/company line and advance cautiously towards the brewery. Upon arrival one squad will detach to sneak up on the southern most fortification and destroy or pin them down for the main force to flank and destroy.
<br/>
<br/>Afterward, all units will move in bounds, clearing buildings and defeating enemy forces as we go. Particular attention will be given to ensuring both flanks remain secure and flanking forces are available to deal with entrenched enemies.
<br/>
<br/>Speed and violence of action will be our greatest assets, ensuring that an enemy that believes itself safe from attack does not get the opportunity to organise a proper counter attack and instead engages friendly forces piece meal.
<br/>
<br/><font color='#99cc00'>Sustainment</font>
<br/>
<br/>No official support exists for this operation, treat casualties on site as best as possible. Additional reinforcements may be sourced from C platoon and sister companies in the form of volunteers.
