<font color='#FFA500' size='18'>Execution</font>
<br/>
<br/><font color='#99cc00'>Commander's Intent</font>
<br/>
<br/>Commander's intent is to establish the teams at their respective objectives and to strike them simultaneously. 
<br/>Following the strikes, teams will regroup and exit the AO to the west.
<br/>
<br/><font color='#99cc00'>Scheme of Maneuvers</font>
<br/>
<br/>Teams must keep to the woods when moving to avoid detection by enemy forces.
<br/>
<br/>Alpha, Bravo and CO will strike the supply depot. Delta and Golf will strike the hotel. 
<br/>
<br/>Charlie, Echo and XO will set up the ambush for the supply convoys.
<br/>
<br/>Teams should recon as much as possible to detect enemy forces while waiting to begin the strikes. Recon units should be used as much as possible to avoid exposing the whole team to the objectives.
<br/>
<br/>The supply depot is most easily approached from the east and north. 
<br/>
<br/>Following the strike, Alpha and Bravo will move south west towards the hotel, and then west towards the ambush site to regroup with the other teams at the ambush site.
<br/>
<br/>Delta and Golf will move west to the ambush site after their strike as well. 
<br/>
<br/>After regrouping, teams will cross the river at the location indicated on the map while covering each other, move south west along the river and then west to leave the AO westwards.
<br/>
<br/>Charlie and Echo should recon from 031005 and set up the first ambush from 031003 or further south, from the east side of the road to avoid detection.
<br/>
<br/>After executing an ambush, Charlie and Echo will set up a new ambush site further up or down the road as the situation allows to prepare for the next convoy without staying in the same position.
<br/>
<br/>In the event of an objective becoming untenable, teams should break contact and move to the next closest objective to regroup with the teams there.
<br/>
<br/><font color='#99cc00'>Scheme of Maneuvers</font>
<br/>
<br/>Teams are outnumbered and outgunned, thus should not engage unless absolutely necessary and keep moving to avoid enemy reinforcements.
<br/>
<br/>Strikes will be executed simultaneously, triggered by the arrival of the first enemy convoy at the ambush site.
<br/>
<br/>When striking the supply depot, Alpha should attempt to move into the location while avoiding detection, plant EOD (fuse timers of 2 minutes or more recommended) and withdraw before detonation. 
<br/>
<br/>Bravo will provide cover. The supply depot also contains many fuel tanks, gas canisters, etc. that can be targeted by explosive weaponry as necessary.
<br/>
<br/>When striking the hotel, Delta and Golf should send AT to attempt to eliminate possible enemy manned vehicles in the parking lot south west of the hotel
<br/>and then immediately attack the buildings from the east and north east to quickly clear the site, eliminate as many enemy officers as possible, and leave before enemy reinforcements arrive.
